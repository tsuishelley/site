I'm a product and design lead that works at the intersection of strategy, systems, and craft. Right now I'm at Curious where I lead product and design across a portfolio of software companies.

I care a lot about making things feel considered. Not precious, just intentional. Whether it's a design system, a product flow, or a website, I want the work to have a point of view.

Outside of work, I'm learning guitar, gardening, and spending time with my two dogs Mei and Yuna along with a very opinionated cat named Totoro (our house is a zoo). My husband Christian and I are slowly making our house feel like ours, one backyard project at a time.

I'm based in the Pacific Northwest and work remotely. If you want to talk about a project, a role, or just compare notes on something you're building I'd love to hear from you.
