I'm a product and design lead working at the intersection of strategy, systems, and craft. Right now I'm at Curious, where I lead product and design across a portfolio of software companies. I've been fortunate to work across a wide range of companies and industries throughout my career. Seeing so many different ways of operating has taught me how to intuit what matters to a product's success and how to execute against it. AI is blurring the lines between disciplines and when you can build anything, what matters most is what you choose to build and how you grow it.

Outside of work, I'm learning how to garden while keeping up with two dogs and one very opinionated cat. I've also started incorporating more analog activities into my life: writing letters, typing on an actual typewriter, and filming the moments that matter on old camcorders.

I'm based in rainy Seattle and work remotely. If you want to talk about a project, a role, or just compare notes on something you're building, I'd love to hear from you.
